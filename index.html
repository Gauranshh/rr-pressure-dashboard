How the score works

Every ball gets a Pressure Index from three things: how much that ball could swing the win probability (35%), how close and how late the game is (35%), and scoreboard stress such as required rate and wickets lost compared with par (30%). Playoff games get a 1.15 boost. The top 25% of balls are pressure balls. Players are rated on those balls only: impact on win probability (35%), quality compared with par for the same situation (25%), rise over their own normal level (15%), consistency across pressure innings (15%) and experience under pressure (10%). Small samples are pulled towards average, weaker opposition counts less, and recent seasons count more.
